About Us

Home to a 46-bay, multi-tier, floodlit driving range, with Trackman technology. Complemented by a practice green and bunker, coffee shop and American Golf Store. There truly is something for everyone as we also boast two outdoor 18-hole dinosaur themed crazy golf courses.

Please note: we are a cashless venue.
